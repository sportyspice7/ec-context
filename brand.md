# Brand — Explore Clairemont

## Overview
The visual identity is bold purple and green with heavy text-overlay graphics. It reads as local, energetic and modern rather than corporate. The same palette runs across the website, the newsletter and social.

## Colors

| Name | Hex | Where it's used |
|---|---|---|
| Purple | `#5E17EB` | Primary brand color — headings, buttons, logo, graphic backgrounds |
| Green | `#3AEB17` | Accent — highlights, callouts, contrast against the purple |

The two are used together, and the pairing is what makes a graphic recognizable at a glance in a feed.

## Typography
**Axiforma** is the brand font.

## Tagline
"Serving The Heart Of San Diego"

## In practice
- Social graphics lean on bold text overlays in the brand colors, not photography alone
- Reels and video are the highest-performing format on Instagram, and Tanja appears on camera in many of them
- The purple/green palette is consistent between the site and social, which is a deliberate recognition play

## Notes for Claude
- When generating any graphic, mockup, slide, chart or HTML page for Explore Clairemont, use `#5E17EB` and `#3AEB17` rather than generic defaults
- Purple leads, green accents. Don't give them equal weight.
- Axiforma may not be available in every context. Fall back to a geometric sans with similar proportions rather than a serif.
- These colors belong to Explore Clairemont, not Gather Locals. Gather Locals is a separate brand.
